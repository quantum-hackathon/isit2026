About the Hackathon

Quantum meets
Information Theory

The ISIT 2026 Quantum Hackathon invites participants to explore the intersection of quantum computing and information theory. Work with quantum simulators, available quantum hardware, and state-of-the-art development kits to create innovative solutions.

Organized by the Student & Outreach Subcommittee of the IEEE Information Theory Society, held in conjunction with the 2026 IEEE International Symposium on Information Theory in Guangzhou, China.

General Objectives

01
Promote Innovation

Create an open platform where participants can explore novel ideas harnessing quantum computing principles to enhance traditional information systems.

02
Encourage Collaborative Learning

Provide a forum for the exchange of project ideas and best practices, ensuring every participant gains insights through shared experiences and collective problem-solving.

03
Develop Holistic Solutions

Inspire teams to build projects that not only advance technological boundaries but also address real-world challenges, benefiting the information theory society as a whole.

Participation

Eligibility &
How to Join

Open to students, researchers, and practitioners worldwide. Whether you're a quantum veteran or just curious — everyone is welcome to apply.

  • ⚡ Team Composition — Teams may consist of three to five members. Please include all team members' names during registration. Individual registrants are warmly welcome — organizers will actively help match individuals into teams to ensure everyone can participate.
  • 🎓 Student Requirement — Each team must have at least two current students enrolled in an academic programme.
  • ◆ IEEE IT Society Membership — For virtual participants, at least one member of the group must be a registered member of the IEEE Information Theory Society at the time of registration.
  • 🌐 Hybrid Format — Participation in ISIT 2026 in person is not required. The hackathon welcomes both remote and in-person participants equally.
  • 💬 Community & Support — Participants will be added to the Slack channel for updates, technical support, and team-matching opportunities. Join #quantum-hackathon-general.
